The big idea

“Boot verbs”

Inside the boot = stem changesnosotros · vosotros = no change

Stem-Changing Verbs

e → ie

When you conjugate these verbs you change the ending and the e in the stem becomes ie — in every form except nosotros (and vosotros).

💡 Remember: draw a line around the forms that change (yo, tú, él/ella, ellos/ellas) and you get a boot. Nosotros stays outside the boot!
Stem-Changing Verbs

o → ue

Same boot pattern. The o in the stem becomes ue; nosotros and vosotros keep the original stem.

⭐ jugar is the only u → ue verb in Spanish. It behaves just like the o → ue verbs: jugar → juego.
El verbo ir

Ir = to go

Ir is completely irregular — no pattern to follow, so learn the six forms by heart.
